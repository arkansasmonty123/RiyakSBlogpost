# PRODUCT POSSIBILITIES — Deferred Items from Roadmap Phases 2‑9

**Status date**: 2026-11-23  
**Project**: THE BAR (single-player legal simulation)  
**Scope**: This document lists every item deferred or blocked across Phases 2‑8, with its current status, the human decision (gate) required to unblock it, and a rough effort estimate. No code is described — only product and planning decisions.

---

## Single‑Player Priority Statement

> THE BAR is a **single‑player** legal simulation. All deferred items are evaluated against the constraint that the game must remain playable and deterministic for one player without external AI‑driven systems making canonical rulings, generating legal text, or synchronising state across users. Any item that introduces multiplayer, server‑authoritative state, or model‑generated legal conclusions is explicitly out of scope unless it can be reduced to deterministic, player‑controlled behaviour. The project's non‑negotiable constraints (no fabricated legal text, no auto‑verification, no RAG over real law, no on‑device model bundling without explicit budget and rights decisions) are assumed for every gate evaluation.

## Notes on Gate Conditions and Effort Estimates

- **Gate condition** always specifies _what human decision_ is required to unblock the item. Many items are blocked on D-005 (legal content owner assignment) or D-011 (versioning policy sign‑off). These are the twin critical-path decisions for the legal‑knowledge track.
- **Effort estimates** use T‑shirt sizing: Low (~1–2 person‑weeks), Medium (~2–4 person‑weeks), Medium‑High (~4–6 person‑weeks), High (~6+ person‑weeks). These are rough planning numbers — actual effort depends on scope refinement and resource availability.
- **Prerequisite chains**: Several items share gates. For example, items #1, #15, #24 all require D-005/D-011 resolution. Item #5 (courtroom) requires P0 validation first. Item #3 (AI provider) requires D-003 + B1 (verified corpus). The dependency graph is implicit in the gate conditions.
- **Single‑player filter**: Any item that would introduce multiplayer synchronization, server‑authoritative state, or model‑generated legal conclusions is marked as out of scope unless it can be reduced to deterministic, player‑controlled behaviour. This filter was applied when classifying each item.
- **Accepted limits**: Items 10‑16, 18‑21, 25‑29, 31 are marked "Deferred (accepted limit)" — these are deferred by intentional design choice, not by external blocker. The project accepts the current limitation and the gate is an internal design decision, not a content‑owner or infrastructure dependency.
