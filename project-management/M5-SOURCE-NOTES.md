## 3) Problem of Induction
- **Framework:** scientific_reasoning | **Format:** scientific_controversy
- **Sources:**
  - Hume, David. *An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding* (1748), Section IV. Public-domain original. pending_review.
  - Popper, Karl. *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* (1959; German original 1934). Discusses induction/falsification. Copyrighted edition - summary only. pending_review.
- **Epistemic:** Philosophical problem about justification of inductive inference. Established: Hume raised the problem; responses contested (Popper, Bayesianism, pragmatism, reliabilism).
- **Cautions:** Do not claim induction is "invalid" or "useless"; state the justification challenge and responses.
